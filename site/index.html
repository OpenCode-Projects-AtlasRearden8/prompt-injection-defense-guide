The cheat sheet

  1. Outside content = data, never instructions.
  2. Fence what you paste; summarize before you act.
  3. The assistant proposes; you dispose.
  4. Two of: imperative verb / authority cosplay / someone gains → hostile till proven boring.
  5. Few keys per assistant. Least privilege, always.
  6. Found a real one? Capture → report → move on.
